   can be extracted.
2. Five proposed exclusions (P006, P028, P037, P155, P156) wait for your decision. P028 is the closest call: the paper calls its
   output "individual olive trees" and compares transformers with CNNs, but it is a pixel-level map evaluated on pixels and sample
   points, not on trees.
3. compile.py warnings: seven P154 values are percentages below 1 (DINOv2 and FoMo-Net mAP50 and mAP50:95, 0.02 to 0.46).
   Table 4 of P154 is in percent (the same columns hold 38.37 and 13.72), confirmed on the rendered p.9, so the values are correct. I
   recorded the seven checks in `compile_review.csv` under a new kind, `percent_below_one`, but compile.py only reads the kind
   `same_value`, so the warnings still print. Either extend compile.py to read the new kind or accept the warnings.
4. P150: Table 4 prints 924.97 for the 5-band parameter count of the Swin Transformer (also in the text, p.13) and the same
   number as its GFLOPs, while the 3-band count is 88 M and the other three backbones differ by 15 to 16 M between 3 and 5 bands. It
   is recorded as printed (V1b); you may want to treat it as a typo in the source. The primary-setting choice for P150 (Table 6
   rather than Table 7) follows protocol 6.3; the headline is the Table 7 result.
5. P154 contributes only its object detection task; its land cover, genus-level classification, grid segmentation (Tables 2 and 3)
   and point cloud experiments (Appendix E, not in this PDF) are area-level or not scored per tree. PolyNet in and out are different
   models, so they are not transfer pairs. Whether Table 4 is on the test split is not stated.
6. P149 does not identify the source of its dataset (Table IV cites high-resolution annotated forest images [3]); its dataset key
   is `forest_health_uav_p149` (kind other). Its text and abstract disagree with its Table III on accuracy (cf items).
7. P023: the text says the four-model fusion with the most detected trees is Swin transformer, faster RCNN, YOLO and DETR, while
   Table 4 lists M1, M2, M3 and M5 (M3 is RCNN). Table 4 is followed.
8. P029 shares the Yosemite data with P005, P014 and P030 (key `yosemite_trees`); it prints a 11.8 cm GSD where P005 prints 0.12 m.
