## Proposed exclusions (author to decide)

Each stays in `papers.csv` as `Pending` with the reason below and is still counted as included until you decide.

| ID | Proposed reason |
|---|---|
| P070 | The only learned model is FastSAM, a YOLOv8-seg based convolutional network trained to imitate SAM, used to segment trunks in RGB images; trunk diameter comes from circle fitting and canopy volume from a convex hull, so no transformer is trained or evaluated (protocol 4.5). |
| P071 | Point cloud completion of 1 m3 tree segments with a fine-tuned PoinTr (a transformer), scored by Chamfer distance and F-score against complete synthetic or real segments; no individual tree is detected, segmented, classified or measured, and the effect of completion on tree height, diameter or volume is named as future work (Sec. 4). |
| P089 | A transformer-based completion network scored by Chamfer distance and F-score on completed point clouds (Tables 1 to 3), and an adversarial augmentation network whose only individual-tree score is the species accuracy of a PointNet++ classifier with attention pooling trained on the augmented data (Table 4: 87.2 original, 88.1 conventional augmentation, 90.1 proposed); the scored classifier is not a transformer and no tree is detected, segmented or measured. |

P089 is the closest call. It scores a species classification of single trees (a task of the review), but the scored classifier is
PointNet++ with attention pooling; the transformer only completes the training samples. If you keep it, the extractable values are the
per-species accuracies of Table 4 (average 87.2, 88.1 and 90.1). P092 is kept although its transformer is also a completion network:
the DBH, height and crown width taken from the completed point clouds are scored against field measurements (R2 0.904, 0.929 and
0.916), so the transformer's output is what the tree scores measure. P071 has no such score (it is named as future work).

## Open points for the author

1. Three proposed exclusions (P070, P071, P089) wait for your decision. P089 is the closest call (see above); P092 stays because the scored tree
   attributes come from the transformer's output, but if you apply a stricter reading (the scored model itself must be the transformer),
   P092 and P089 would be judged together.
2. Scope: P068 and P086 extract tree points from urban multispectral LiDAR (P086 has no individual trees at all, only a binary semantic
   segmentation); P073, P079, P088, P093 and P095 separate wood and leaf (or trunk, branch and leaf) in single-tree point clouds; P075, P080,
   P084 and P092 estimate tree attributes. All are recorded under the tags of protocol 6 (`component_segmentation` at point level, or
   `attribute_estimation`); decide whether the point-level and component papers stay in the review.
3. Dataset keys that rest on citations or assumptions: P093 (`stpctls`, `lewos`, see above); P081 and P095 use ForestSemantic of Liang et al.
   (Evo, Finland; Zenodo 15080627; key `forestsemantic_evo`) while P063 uses ForestSemantic-MS (`forestsemantic_ms`), which the registry
   keeps as another dataset; merge the keys if the two share the plots. P094's ten plots are the ALS dataset that P066 cites as Xi et al.
   (2024); P066 uses four of them (LPine 1, 4, 6 and RPine).
4. P084 prints three different sets of height errors (abstract, text, Table 2); five cf items record the choices (Table 2 and the text over the
   abstract). The text value of the D3 RMSE (0.21 m) cannot be right next to a MAE of 0.37 m and is not recorded.
5. P081: the Table 2 values average all six plots, including the training plot of each model, so they are not clean test results (split
   `not_stated`); the ForAINet comparison and the mIoU of the prompt mode (Fig. 3, Table 3) are not recorded.
6. P095: its transfer pair compares the RubberTree test mIoU (88.78) with new point clouds of the same plantation city (86.15); the paper does
   not say what differs, so the shift type `site` is a weak label. The R2 column of its Table 3 is not recorded (the quantity is not defined
   clearly), and the R2 of the field test is only printed in the abstract (1.00, 0.99, 0.89).
7. The text layers of P096 (tables and prose overlaid), P092 and P073 (partly duplicated) are poor. The P096 table values are typed from the
   rendered page (a scan of the same table by OCR found them all); check the quotes of those papers against the rendered pages if you
   spot-check.
