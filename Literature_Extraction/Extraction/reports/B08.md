## Proposed exclusions

None. Every B08 paper trains or scores a transformer-based model (a transformer baseline is enough, as the screening criteria say) and
reports a quantitative result for trees, crowns or dead tree pixels.

Closest calls among the papers that were kept (not proposed; recommendations are in section 0 of `decisions.md`):

- P137: the only scored learned model output is the street tree detection of Table 1 (Mask2Former is scored and has the best F1); the
  vitality index (0.7 NDVI plus 0.3 NDMI) is computed per tree without a reference and is not scored, the paper chooses U-Net for the
  vitality step, and neither the split nor the scored unit (pixel or tree) is stated. It is the paper of B08 closest to having no evaluated
  individual-tree output.
- P140: one SegFormer-B5 model (no baseline) segments standing deadwood (dead crowns and partial canopy dieback) as pixels; the labels are
  crowd-sourced and partly circles. It belongs to the scope group of pixel-level crown condition masks (P125, P129, P130, P131, P133, P142, P151, P152,
  P135).
- P143: the multimodal router (Gemini or GPT-4o) only selects a segmentation model; the scored dead tree output is a polygon matched by any
  overlap, so a polygon can be a group of dead trees (level `instance_mask`); the transformers SegFormer and Mask2Former are baselines
  inside the pipelines.
- P131, P136 and P144: the transformer is only a baseline or comparison model (a fine-tuned SAM, DETR, Deformable DETR) and the proposed
  or best model is a CNN. That is enough under the screening criterion that transformers used only as baselines count.
