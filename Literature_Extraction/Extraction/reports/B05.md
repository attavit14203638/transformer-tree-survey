## Proposed exclusions (author to decide)

Each stays in `papers.csv` as `Pending` with the reason below and is still counted as included until you decide.

| ID | Proposed reason |
|---|---|
| P038 | The four evaluated models are Mask R-CNN variants with ResNet50 or ResNet101 backbones (CNNs that differ in pre-training); SAM-HQ (ViT-H) is used only as a semi-automatic annotation tool and is neither trained nor scored. |
| P046 | CCD-YOLO is a YOLOv8 variant whose modules are convolutional: CReToNeXt (re-parameterized convolutions and residual connections, a transformer only in its name), CBAM, and a dynamic head with convolution-based scale, spatial and task attention; no multi-head self-attention block is described, so no transformer is trained or evaluated (protocol 4.5). |
| P055 | SPGN is a graph convolution network with a scale pyramid module and positional encoding but no transformer block; SAM appears only in a qualitative visual comparison (Fig. 13) with no scored result, and the scored comparison models of Table IV (Mask R-CNN, SS-FPN and SOLO variants) are convolutional. |

P046 is the closest call: the abstract calls CReToNeXt a "cross residual transformer network", and the dynamic head is described as a
"self-attention mechanism". If you keep it, the extractable values are in Tables 2 (crown detection and boundary segmentation counts)
and 3 (box and mask precision, recall, F1 and AP50 for the module ablation; the proposed row is 81.2, 74.1, 77.5, 82.9 for boxes and
82.4, 72.8, 77.9, 83.0 for masks).

Other choices that are not cf items but follow protocol 6 and are written in the `notes` field of each paper:

- P039 and P054: both tables label a column mIoU while the paper's formula is the IoU of the target class. P039's printed mIoU is
  recorded as `iou` (primary, as the formula says; its values do not follow the printed formulas, see its notes). P054's is recorded
  as `miou` and is not primary: its values equal the mean of the canopy IoU implied by F1 and the background IoU implied by OA and F1
  (0.9345 against 0.9346 for the proposed 3-D row), so they include the background class. F1 (canopy class) is primary.
- P041: the primary results are challenge set 1 (the first listed test set); the validation set is the in-domain value of the 12 transfer pairs.
- P043: the primary setting is Jekyll (first dataset), ADE20k pre-training, RGB input; Table V (no pre-training) is a separate model item.
- P045: Table 6 is not primary. FM-SAM's printed precision (92.45) and recall (91.81) equal the means of the five per-class values of
  Table 2 (four species and the background), so the overall scores include a non-tree class (protocol 6.3). The BAMFORESTS
  transfer pairs use the Overall rows of Table 7 against the Table 6 scores; whether the models were retrained on BAMFORESTS is not
  stated, the paper calls it unseen data.
- P047: OA, mIoU and kappa are overall scores of two classes, so nothing is primary. The ablation baseline's kappa of -0.003 (Table 4)
  is not recorded because compile.py only accepts kappa from 0 to 1.
- P053: the ratio of segmented to true trees is metric `other`; the "Accuracy rate" rows equal the mean of the five plot ratios but the paper
  does not say so (aggregation not stated).
- P059: Table 1 is printed with decimal commas; mAP and mAP at IoU 0.5 are taken from the results text, which prints the same values with
  points. TreePseCo and DF (FT) are trained on the Valle d'Aosta data, so only the original DeepForest gives transfer pairs.
- P061: the primary settings (F1 and mIoU of the crown masks, R2 and RMSE of the crown area) all use the RGB input at IoU 0.5; F1 at IoU 0.75
  and the mean over 0.50 to 0.95 are recorded without `primary`; boundary F1 is metric `other`.
- P066: the UAV dataset is primary (first listed); the hands-off TreeisoNet is the fully automatic result, the intervened one is a
  separate model item. ForAINet's numbers are marked `copied_from` Xiang et al. (2024), which is not in the corpus.
- P067: the paper calls its mIoU instance-level but defines it over m classes, and does not say which trees the deep learning baselines

## Open points for the author

1. Three proposed exclusions (P038, P046, P055) wait for your decision. P046 is the closest call (see above): "transformer" is in
   the name of its CReToNeXt module, but the described blocks are convolutional.
2. P045: the paper's Table 2 prints a Sophora japonica recall of 91.83 where its text and Table 3 give 89.83 (the F1 of 92.73 fits
   89.83), and its text gives a Populus nigra precision of 92.29 against 92.92 in Table 2. Neither value is recorded (per-species
   scores are not recorded). The overall scores of Table 6 include the background, which is why none of its results is primary; if
   you prefer to keep them primary, change the tag in `scripts/B05/P045.py`.
3. compile.py cannot take a negative kappa (P047 baseline, -0.003, left out). Either extend the range check to -1 to 1 for kappa or accept
   the omission.
4. P059 uses its own dataset key `neon_aop_rgb_p059` for its NEON data (0.15 m RGB, over 30,000 boxes) because the paper does not say
   that it is the NeonTreeEvaluation benchmark (key `neon_treeevaluation`, used by P050); merge the keys if you know they are the same data.
   The resolution of its Valle d'Aosta orthophotos is missing from the printed text ("cm/pixel resolution").
5. P050 prints "up to 0.38 at specific sites" in the abstract, but its Table 3 has site values up to 0.415 (WREF, FA local); only the
   Average row is recorded.
6. P066: the ForAINet numbers are copied from Xiang et al. (2024), which is not in the corpus; text and tables disagree for four averages
   (cf items). The UAV, TLS and ALS plots come from public benchmarks; the UAV data are the original FOR-instance (new key
   `for_instance_v1`), not FOR-instanceV2.
7. Scope notes: P043 uses street-view and smartphone photographs, P049 and P065 close-range images (P065 segments leaves and branches
   for pruning, task `component_segmentation`), P064, P066 and P067 are point clouds. All are recorded under the tags of protocol 6;
   decide whether the close-range and component papers stay in the review.
8. P052 (20 networks) and P054 (18 networks over Tables II, V and VII) compare many networks; only the models required by the skill
   (proposed, transformer-based, best CNN, the baseline it builds on) plus the ones the text discusses are recorded.
