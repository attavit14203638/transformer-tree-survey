## Proposed exclusion (author to decide)

It stays in `papers.csv` as `Pending` with the reason below and is still counted as included until you decide.

| ID | Proposed reason |
|---|---|
| P097 | PointRes replaces the self-attention of Point Transformer with ResMLP layers, and it and the Point Transformer baseline are scored by mAcc and mIoU over all classes of S3DIS (indoor rooms, Table 2) and DALES (eight classes, vegetation being one class, Table 3); tree barriers are vegetation points near power lines, so no individual tree is delineated, detected, counted or measured. |

If you keep P097, the only numbers are the class-averaged mAcc and mIoU of Tables 2 and 3 (PointRes 71.6 mIoU on S3DIS, abstract); the
paper has no tree-specific score, and its tree barrier reports (Table 4) list coordinates of dangerous vegetation without an accuracy.

Closest calls among the papers that were kept (not proposed, but they would fall together under a stricter reading of protocol 4.5, where
the scored model itself must be the transformer, or of the scope, where scored tree values must be field data):

- P109: all species classification is done by ResUnet CNNs; SAM ViT-H is only used zero-shot to delineate crowns and is scored by a boundary F1
  (98 percent). It is kept because SAM is evaluated and is part of the tree extraction. Compare P038 (B05, proposed), where SAM-HQ was only an
  annotation tool and was not scored.
- P098: a completion network; its tree-level scores (RMSE of height, DBH and crown widths) use reference values computed from the real
  (complete) point clouds, not from field measurements. It is kept as P092 (B06) was, but its reference is weaker than P092's field data.
  Compare P071 and P089 (B06, proposed).
- P125: the proposed RCANet is a CNN; the transformers (SegFormer and Swin-UNet) are baselines, which is enough under protocol 4.5, but the
  proposed model is not a transformer (the same holds for P104, where ViT is a comparison classifier, and for P103, where SAM is one stage).

   P103 and P104, where the proposed model is not a transformer but SAM or ViT is a scored stage or comparison model.

   papers stay in the review.
